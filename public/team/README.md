# Team photos

Put each member's photo here, then add `photo: "/team/<file>"` to that member in
`src/content/site.ts` (`team.members`). Until a photo is added, the site shows initials and
"Photo coming soon".

Rules:

- Square (1:1), at least 400 × 400 px.
- JPG or WebP, under 150 KB.
- Plain, simple background; face clearly visible.
- `.webp` is fine too: just use that extension in the file name and in `site.ts`.
